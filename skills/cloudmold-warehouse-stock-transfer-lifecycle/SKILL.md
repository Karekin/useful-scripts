# Warehouse stock-transfer lifecycle

Use this R3 workflow when a warehouse transfer must be visible as a formal request, order, outbound batch, and receipt batch. The workflow is the sole authority for the related inventory dispatch and receipt ledger entries; never insert transfer or inventory records directly.

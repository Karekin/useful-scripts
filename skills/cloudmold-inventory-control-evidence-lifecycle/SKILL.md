# Inventory control evidence lifecycle

Use the R3 task definition to publish a versioned safety-stock policy and persist inventory health, aging, and expiry snapshots from canonical ledger evidence. Provide exact owner, SKU, network, policy, and snapshot identities; do not calculate or persist substitute projections outside the owning services.
